Bora pro Treino! is a gym logbook made to be quick between sets.

Open today's workout, see what you did last time and log the set in one tap. One button says exactly what it will log, like "Log 125 kg × 8". If today is different, change the weight with the big minus and plus buttons. No keyboard, no "start workout" step, no account.

LOGGING IN ONE TAP

• Today shows your next routine, with a continue button and a progress ring. The training finishes by itself when the last exercise is done.

• Tired day? "Same as last time" logs the whole routine as you did it last time, in one tap, with undo. Or use the suggested weights.

• Sets by weight and reps, by time (plank, 30 s farmer's walk) or by distance, plus warm-up suggestions before your heavy sets.

• Cardio: minutes, calories, distance and intensity (decimals too, like 7.5). Use the steppers, or tap a number to enter it on a big number pad.

• Rest timer that rings on time even with the screen off, with a countdown notification and an optional beep.

ROUTINES AND AGENDA

• Build routine A, B, C and more, with sets and rep targets. Train them in turn on the days you choose, or on fixed weekdays.

• The calendar shows what you trained, what you planned and which routine comes next in the rotation. Plan several workouts on a day, keep a weekly goal and a streak.

• Forgot to log? Add or fix a past training.

• A library of about 750 exercises with how-to instructions and filters by muscle group, equipment and level. Or create your own.

PROGRESS

• An overview that starts with what matters now: a muscle group with few sets, an exercise stuck at the same weight, a new record. Tap to see the details.

• A chart and trend for each exercise, records, sets per muscle group, a body map, and a detailed analysis for those who like numbers.

• Body measurements (waist, hips, chest, arm and more) and your weight, with their trend.

• Training summary with a picture you can share.

ALSO

• A home screen widget that shows your next workout.

• Training reminders that tell you what is next.

• Optional Health Connect: sends each finished training there. Off by default, it only writes and never reads.

• Backup and restore with a simple file, to move your history to a new phone.

• English and Brazilian Portuguese (Baianês).

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app does not even ask for internet access.
