Bora pro Treino! is a gym logbook made to be quick between sets.

Open today's workout, see what you lifted last time and log the set in one tap. One button says exactly what it will log, like "Log 125 kg × 8". If today is different, change the weight with the big minus and plus buttons. No typing, no "start workout" step, no account.

WHAT YOU GET

• Today: your next routine, whether your training follows a rotation or fixed weekdays (you set that in the Agenda tab), with a start or continue button and a progress ring.

• One-tap logging: the button logs the set on the screen. Warm-up suggestions tell you what to lift before your heavy sets.

• Your gym, registered once: add your machines and exercises, or pick from a library of about 750 exercises. Each one has instructions on how to do it, and you can filter by muscle group, equipment and level. Or create your own. Machines from the library show how to do them.

• Routines (fichas): build routine A, B, C and more, with sets and rep targets. After a training, a routine can be updated to match what you did, in one tap, with undo. A training finishes by itself when the last exercise in its routine is done.

• Rest timer that keeps working with the screen off, with a countdown notification and a buzz when it is time for the next set.

• Agenda: a week strip shows the workouts you trained and the ones you planned. Plan several workouts per day and reorder them. Keep track of your weekly goal and your streak of weeks. Forgot to log something? Add or fix a past training.

• Progress, Exercises page: a sparkline for each exercise and how much it changed.

• Progress, Stats: split into Body, Strength and Habit, with highlights. The body map shows front and back, with direct and indirect sets for each muscle group. Also strength per body weight, balance between muscle groups, records and rep ranges.

• Training summary with an image you can share.

• Cardio too: log minutes, calories and distance for the treadmill, bike and more.

• Optional Health Connect: send each finished training to Health Connect. It is off by default, it only writes, and the app never reads from it.

• Training reminders that tell you what is next.

• Backup and restore with a simple file, to move your history to a new phone.

• First-run setup in 3 steps.

• English and Brazilian Portuguese (Baianês), with a light look.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything stays on your phone (the Health Connect option only writes to your own Health Connect). There is no account, no ads, no tracking and the app does not even ask for internet access.
