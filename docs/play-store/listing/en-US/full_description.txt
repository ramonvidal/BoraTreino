Bora pro Treino! is a simple gym logbook made to be quick between sets.

Open a machine, see what you lifted last time and log today's set in one tap. Change the weight with big minus and plus buttons. No typing, no "start workout" step, no account.

WHAT YOU GET

• One-tap logging: "Same again" repeats last time's set, "Log set" saves the numbers on the screen.

• Your gym, registered once: add every machine and exercise your gym has (pick from a list of about 100, many with a picture, or create your own) and keep setup notes like "seat 4".

• Routines (fichas): build routine A, B, C and more from your machines, with sets and rep targets. The app tells you which routine is next, whatever day of the week it is, so training four days a week just works.

• Progress that tells you when to go up: when you reach the top of your rep range on every set, the machine suggests the next weight. New records get a little celebration.

• Rest timer after each set, with a countdown notification and a buzz when it is time for the next one.

• Calendar of your training days with a weekly goal and a streak of weeks. Forgot to log something? Add or fix a past training.

• Progress charts for every machine: your top weight over 3 months, 1 year or all time.

• Cardio too: log minutes, calories and distance for the treadmill, bike and more, and see your week.

• Optional Health Connect: send each finished training to Health Connect. It is off by default and the app never reads from it.

• Training reminders that tell you what is next.

• Backup and restore with a simple file, to move your history to a new phone.

• English and Brazilian Portuguese (Baianês), light and dark mode.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Everything stays on your phone (the Health Connect option only writes to your own Health Connect). There is no account, no ads, no tracking and the app does not even ask for internet access.
